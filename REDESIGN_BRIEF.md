# Antiheroism Redesign Brief

## Recommended Outline

1. Navigation
   - Brand wordmark
   - Anchors to manifesto, ventures, and contact
2. Hero
   - A sharp positioning statement
   - One paragraph explaining the studio thesis
   - Primary and secondary CTAs
   - A short "current thesis" panel that signals taste and direction
3. Manifesto
   - Why Antiheroism exists
   - What kinds of products it backs
   - The studio point of view in plain language
4. Operating Principles
   - Start at the edges
   - Make the thesis legible
   - Earn conviction through shipping
5. Ventures
   - One live company
   - One in-progress company
   - Each card should show stage, category, short thesis, and CTA
6. Contact
   - Who should reach out
   - What to include in the intro
   - Clear email CTA
7. Footer
   - One-line brand summary
   - Minimal utility links

## Content Direction

- Sound opinionated, not corporate.
- Prefer specificity over broad startup language.
- Keep the focus on taste, clarity, and user behavior.
- Make Antiheroism feel small by choice, not small by lack of progress.

## Why This Structure Works

- The old page had attitude, but not enough explanation.
- The shipped Next page had structure, but not enough personality.
- The new structure combines both: first a memorable stance, then proof, then a clean path to contact.

## Copy Notes For Future Iteration

- If more ventures ship, keep the portfolio section tight and curated.
- If the studio starts investing externally, add a section explaining the model.
- If founder outreach becomes important, add a short founder profile or process strip before contact.
